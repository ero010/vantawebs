Tips for a convincing comparison

Shoot from the same angle. Stand in the same spot for the after shot — matching framing makes the transformation undeniable. Use 4:5 for feeds and 9:16 Story for statuses. Keep the divider near the middle so both sides get equal glory, and leave labels on so viewers instantly know which side is which — even in a tiny WhatsApp preview.

Frequently asked questions

What is a before/after photo?

One image split by a draggable divider showing the before state on one side and the after state on the other — visual proof of cleaning, detailing, painting or renovation work.

My photos are different sizes. Will it still work?

Yes. Both photos are auto-cropped to fill the same frame. Pick match, square, 4:5 or 9:16 depending on where you will post.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser with JavaScript. Your photos never leave your device.

Can I share straight to WhatsApp?

Yes. On phones the Share button opens the native share sheet — send to WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere else in one tap.
