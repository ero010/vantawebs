What is hidden in your files?

Every photo you take embeds EXIF data: your camera model, the exact GPS coordinates where it was shot, the date and time, lens settings, and sometimes your name. PDFs carry author names and editing software. Videos store device info and location. AI-generated images now carry C2PA Content Credentials and generator parameters. Vantawebs strips all of it — losslessly, in your browser, for free.

Frequently asked questions

Do my files get uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser with JavaScript. Disconnect from the internet after loading the page and the tool still works.

Will this reduce my image quality?

No. Metadata is removed at the binary level without re-encoding pixels. Output is pixel-identical.

Does it remove AI-generation metadata?

Yes — C2PA Content Credentials, IPTC Digital Source Type tags, and embedded parameters from Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion and Adobe Firefly.

What formats are supported?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF, TIFF, PDF, MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, MP3 and WAV. Free tier: up to 10 files per batch.
