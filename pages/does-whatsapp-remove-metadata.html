Does WhatsApp Remove Photo Metadata?

It depends entirely on how you hit send — and the wrong choice leaks your GPS. When you send an image in WhatsApp as a Photo (via the Gallery/Camera picker), WhatsApp compresses it, resizes it, and strips EXIF/GPS before delivery. The recipient gets a clean but visibly degraded copy. That is the behavior most people assume always applies. It does not.

When you send the same image as a Document (Attach > Document > choose the file), WhatsApp delivers the byte-for-byte original — every EXIF tag, GPS coordinate, timestamp, device ID, and thumbnail intact. People choose Document precisely when they want full quality for printing or archiving, which is exactly when they accidentally broadcast where and when the photo was taken and on what device. Marketplace sellers, landlords, and parents share originals as documents every day without realizing it.

  • Sent as Photo — compressed, resized, EXIF/GPS stripped; safe but lossy
  • Sent as Document — original file preserved with ALL metadata including GPS; the big surprise leak
  • HD photo option — higher quality but still processed; do not assume it equals a raw original
